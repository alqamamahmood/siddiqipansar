Privacy Policy — Siddiqipansar

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Siddiqipansar mobile application and the SiddiqiPansar online store (together, the "Service") collect, use and protect your information. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

Siddiqipansar is an online store for authentic pansar herbal products, desi medicines and natural oils. The mobile app presents our store so you can browse products and place orders conveniently from your phone.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to run the store and fulfil your orders:

  • Order and contact details — your name, phone number, email address and delivery address, which you provide when placing an order or contacting us.
  • Order information — the products you order, order history and payment method details needed to complete your purchase. Payments are processed through the payment methods offered at checkout; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Device and usage information — basic technical information (such as device type, browser/app version and pages visited) that is automatically collected when you use the app or website, used to keep the Service working and secure.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, pack and deliver your orders.
  • To contact you about your order, delivery or support request.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the store and the app.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Sharing of information

Your information is shared only where necessary to provide the Service:

  • With delivery/courier services, so your order can reach your address.
  • With payment providers, to process your payment securely.
  • When required by law or a lawful request from authorities.

5. Cookies

Our store website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences and understand how the store is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Order and contact details are kept only for as long as needed to fulfil orders, provide support and meet legal requirements.

7. Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request its deletion. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless we are required by law to keep it.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
